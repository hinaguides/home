What this drawing is based on

  1. Plot shape built from the society map's own numbers: B1 39.80, B2 43.40, R1 53.68, F2 25.40, F1 21.40, C1 8.71, R2 40.76 and the middle line 49.92 (square to the back). Edge angles were read off the map. C2 is not printed on the map; it works out to 7.94 ft.
  2. Plot area 3,486 sq ft, which matches 14 marla at about 250 sq ft per marla (5 marla = 25 × 50).
  3. Setbacks as you gave them: 10 ft front, 5 ft back. Side roads (R1, R2) taken as zero; corner plots often need one, so confirm with New City.
  4. Ground coverage here is 59% of the plot. Check the society's maximum coverage.
  5. Walls: 9" brick for outer and main walls, 4½" for bathroom and wardrobe partitions. Plaster and finishes are not deducted from room sizes.
  6. Stair: dog-leg, two 3½ ft flights, 10" treads, for about 11 ft floor-to-floor. A lockable door goes at the upper landing.
  7. Columns, beams and foundations are not shown. A structural engineer places them; expect 9" × 12" or 12" × 12" columns inside the 9" walls, sized for a future third floor.
  8. North is taken as the top of the society map.
