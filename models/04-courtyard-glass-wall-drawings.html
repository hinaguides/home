Courtyard glass wall — elevation and section

The full-glass wall each room shows to the courtyard, split into zones: openable vents on top, folding doors and fixed glass below, curtain track at the transom bar, gallery roof as the sunshade.

Front view from the courtyard

Side cut with sun angles

  • Overhang: the gallery roof stops steep summer sun before it reaches the glass; low winter sun slips under and warms the room. Works best on glass facing south to southeast.
  • Hopper vent: tilts in from a bottom hinge. Warm air escapes over the top; rain runs off the outside.
  • Double glass: 2 panes with an air gap, in doors and vents.
  • Mesh: on the courtyard side, outside the glass. Pleated on the folding doors, fixed on the vents.
  • Curtain track: at the transom bar, so closed curtains never block the vents.
