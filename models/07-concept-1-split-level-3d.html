How this house works

One heart, downstairs

Kitchen, dining and living exist only on the ground floor, wrapped around the lawn. Upstairs has a tea bar, not a kitchen, so everyone eats together. Your parents' room opens onto the same lawn as the great room, next to it, not below everyone.

Two doors, L parking

Car 1 parks along R1 and opens into the stair tower, which has its own R1 door. Car 2 enters from the front and opens into the foyer beside the main door. Both sit behind plain shutters.

Rooms without a hotel corridor

The tower holds a spiral stair. Room A opens off its first landing, Room B across a short bridge over the courtyard, the loft at the very top. Every door is reached from a different place and height.

Lock two doors to rent

Switch to Rented. The tower's glass door to the courtyard and the bridge door into the sky lounge lock. Tenants get the R1 door, the tower and three rooms with baths. The family keeps everything else.

Rentable rooms in Rented mode

Informal corners

Dining two steps up from the living. A tea court with a tree between the parents' room, office and kitchen. A window seat on the stair landing. Sky lounge with a reading daybed, standing desk and a balcony over the lawn. Terrace with a charpai, pergola and gym pavilion. Roof deck by the loft.

Check before going further

The loft makes a third level: confirm height and floor limits with New City (question 4). The R1 shutter and door depend on question 1. Labels show gross areas including baths and walls. Sun is drawn from the south-west, afternoon.
