How this house works

Rule 1: noise stays at the front. Living room, dining, kitchen, car porch and the upper lounge sit in a loud band along the front road. Your parents' room, office and bedrooms sit in a quiet band along the back. The courtyard, stairs, baths and stores sit between as a buffer. Turn on Noise zones to see it.

One gate, at F1

Only the front has openings. One gate beside F1 opens into a covered porch for 2 cars. From the porch: the main door to the foyer, a side door to the kitchen, and the stair door. R1 and R2 are solid walls with no windows, doors or garage.

Inside courtyard

A lawn about 12 x 30 ft behind a 13 ft wall on R1, invisible from the street. Living room, dining, parents' room and office open onto it.

Back terrace

Walled on the neighbours' side. Glass on the other 3 sides: your room, the reading gallery and the kids' room.

House used as a whole

Upstairs has no kitchen, so everyone comes down to eat. The stair has its own door from the porch and a lockable door to the back hall, so Rooms C and D can be let without the family moving out.

Rooms

Ground: living, dining, kitchen, back hall with laundry, maid's room, gym, pantry, utility, parents' room, office, WC, store. First: your room, kids' room, Rooms C and D, reading gallery, upper lounge, 2 baths. No prayer room.

Weak spots to fix next

Maid's room has no outside wall and needs a skylight. The gym sits under the kids' room, so it needs a rubber floor. Room D shares a wall with the upper lounge; its bath and wardrobe sit on that wall as a buffer.

Levels: ground 0 ft, first +11.25 ft. Setbacks 5 ft front and back. North from the satellite view: front faces SSE.
