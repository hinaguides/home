## Concept 2 — noise zoned (9 Nov 2026)

Model: [`models/08-concept-2-noise-zoned-3d.html`](../models/08-concept-2-noise-zoned-3d.html)

Why it changed: in Concept 1 the living room sat in the middle and spread noise to every room around it. The society also allows no openings or garage on R1 or R2, and the R2-side terrace wasn't wanted.

How it works:
- **Front band (loud):** living room (front R1 corner, opens onto the courtyard), foyer, covered car porch for 2 cars, kitchen, dining. Upstairs: upper lounge (play, tea bar).
- **Middle band (buffer):** courtyard about 12 x 30 ft, back hall with laundry, maid's room, stair, pantry, WC, utility, store. Upstairs: reading gallery, 2 baths, hall.
- **Back band (quiet):** parents' room and office on the courtyard. Upstairs: your room, back terrace, kids' room, Room C.
- **One gate beside F1.** From the porch: main door to the foyer, side door to the kitchen, door to the stair.
- **R1 and R2 solid.** Courtyard wall on R1 is 13 ft. Terrace at the back centre, long wall on the neighbours' side, glass from 3 rooms.
- **Used as a whole:** no kitchen upstairs. Stair has its own porch door and a lockable door to the back hall, so Rooms C and D can be let.

Weak spots:
- Maid's room has no outside wall and needs a skylight.
- Gym sits under the kids' room and needs a rubber floor.
- Room D shares a wall with the upper lounge; its bath and wardrobe form the buffer.

## Concept 1 — split levels and stair tower (8 Nov 2026)

Model: [`models/07-concept-1-split-level-3d.html`](../models/07-concept-1-split-level-3d.html). **Breaks rules 1 and 2**, kept for ideas.

Brief from Ehsan that day:
- Introverted family that rarely hosts; relatives move freely.
- Parents shouldn't seem alone downstairs; the house should be used as a whole but be separable if rented.
- Rentable rooms each with their own entrance and bath, no hotel-style corridor.
- Lots of informal corners; not a square box.

What it tried:
- Lawn courtyard on R1, great room (living plus dining raised 2 steps) and parents' room on it.
- Open-to-sky tea court between parents' room, office and kitchen.
- L parking: car 1 along R1 with an R1 shutter, car 2 from the front.
- Stair tower with its own R1 door and a spiral stair. Room A off the first landing, Room B across a bridge over the courtyard, roof loft at the top.
- Informal corners: window seat on the stair landing, sky lounge with balcony, terrace with charpai and pergola, roof deck.

Ideas worth keeping: rooms reached from different places instead of a corridor, informal corners, the lockable links between family and let rooms.

## Ideas from the 8 Nov chat that still stand

- **Room list for modern living:** home office (2 desks, 1 standing), small gym, live-in maid's room with its own bath, big store, utility corner (inverter, pump, water tank), shoe niche at each entrance, EV charging point in the porch, ground-floor bedroom for parents with an accessible bath.
- **Dining:** one shared dining table in an open space, used by family daily and by guests. Extendable, 6 seats daily, 10 to 12 for guests.
- **Two kitchens or one:** a shared kitchen is the most common friction between 2 families. The current plan keeps one kitchen on purpose so the house is used as a whole. If the upper floor ever becomes a family unit, leave gas, water and drain points upstairs so a kitchen can be added later.
